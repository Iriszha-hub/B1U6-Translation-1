Translation: Taijiquan

汉译英专项训练 · 短句翻译 (Unit 6)

一、从短句翻译到段落翻译

四六级考试考查的是段落翻译。

从短句翻译过渡到段落翻译，需要关注两个层面：

  • 句内：句子内部如何组织？（主语 + 谓语 + 宾语？主系表？）
  • 句间：句子与句子之间如何衔接？（并列？转折？因果？）

翻译的基本单位是句子，而非单词或短语。

因此，要译好段落，须先译好句子；要译好句子，须先理解英语句子的结构以及句与句之间的连接词。

二、逐句精讲

句子 1

太极拳是一种武术，也是一种健身运动，在中国有着悠久的历史。

句式：主系表结构，并列句

主干：Taijiquan + is + a martial art and a fitness exercise；It + has + a long history

时态：一般现在时（is）+ 一般现在时（has）

Taijiquan is a martial art and a fitness exercise as well. It has a long history in China.

重点词汇：

martial art 武术｜fitness exercise 健身运动｜as well 也；还｜a long history 悠久的历史

重点句型：A is B and C as well.（A既是B也是C）

句子 2

太极拳动作缓慢而柔和，适合任何年龄、性别、体型（build）的人。

句式：主系表结构，介词短语作状语

主干：Taijiquan + is + suitable for + anyone

难点：“动作缓慢而柔和”处理为 With 引导的伴随状语；“任何年龄、性别、体型”用 whatever 引导的让步状语从句处理。

With slow and gentle movements, Taijiquan is suitable for anyone, whatever their age, gender, or build.

重点词汇：

movement n. 动作｜gentle adj. 柔和的｜be suitable for 适合……｜gender n. 性别｜build n. 体型

重点句型：whatever + 名词（无论什么……）

句子 3

太极拳既可用于防身（self-defense），又能强身健体，因而深受中国人民和世界人民的喜爱。

句式：被动语态 + 并列结构 + 因果关系

主干：Taijiquan + can be used for... as well as...；Therefore, it + has become popular

时态：情态动词被动语态（can be used）+ 现在完成时（has become）

Taijiquan can be used for self-defense as well as building one's body. Therefore, it has become very popular among people both in China and around the world.

重点词汇：

self-defense n. 防身｜as well as 既……又……（连接并列成分）｜therefore adv. 因此｜popular adj. 受欢迎的

重点句型：A as well as B（既A又B）；Therefore, ...（因此，……）

三、四级段落翻译真题实战

真题段落（2024.6）：
农历是中国传统文化的重要组成部分，当今依然广为使用。它不仅指导农业生产，还指导人们的日常生活。农历在中国有着悠久的历史，它是中国古代劳动人民智慧的结晶。
The lunar calendar is a significant part of traditional Chinese culture which is still widely used today. It guides agricultural production as well as people's daily lives. The lunar calendar has a long history in China, and it is the crystallization of the wisdom of the ancient Chinese working people.

连接词拆解：
1. which：定语从句，连接农历与文化。
2. as well as：并列连词，连接农业和日常生活。
3. and：并列连词，连接“历史悠久”与“劳动人民的结晶”。
真题段落（2023.12）：
中国政府十分重视人民的健康饮食。近年来，中国政府通过加大资金投入和政策引导，促进了健康饮食的发展。中国将进一步完善健康饮食体系，以保障人民的身体健康。
The Chinese government attaches great importance to the people's healthy diet. In recent years, the government has promoted the development of a healthy diet by increasing financial input and policy guidance. China will further improve the healthy diet system so as to ensure people's physical health.

连接词拆解：
1. by：介词，表示方式方法。
2. so as to：目的状语，连接“完善体系”与“保障健康”。
真题段落（2025.6）：
超级杂交水稻营养丰富，口感更佳。它不仅大幅提高了粮食产量，还有效缓解了世界饥饿问题。
Super hybrid rice is rich in nutrition and has a better taste. Not only does it greatly increase grain output, but it also effectively alleviates the problem of world hunger.

连接词拆解：
1. and：并列连词，连接营养与口感。
2. Not only... but also：不仅……而且（由于 Not only 置于句首，引起了倒装 does it）。

四、补充材料：英语句间逻辑连接词（九大类·全注解版）

并列：

【例句 1】Many martial arts, such as Taijiquan and Shaolin Kungfu, are popular.
【例句 2】He is a good student. A case in point is his perfect score in the final exam.
【例句 3】He is too young for the job. In other words, he is not qualified.
【例句 4】To illustrate, let us look at the chart below.

表恶化（更糟的是）：

【例句 1】It is not only good for health but also helps relax the mind.
【例句 2】The house is too expensive; what is more, it is far from the city center.

【例句 1】Taijiquan is good for health. Therefore, it is popular.

【例句 1】Although it is simple to make, it is of great significance.
【例句 2】He looks very young. In fact, he is already over 40. (转折：看似...实际上)
【例句 3】Regardless of the heavy rain, the rescue team continued their search.
【例句 4】Admittedly, the task is difficult, but we must complete it.
【例句 5】He is very generous, whereas his brother is quite mean.
